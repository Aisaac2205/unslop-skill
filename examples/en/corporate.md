# Corporate examples: English

## Example 1: Web CTA block

### Before
> DISCOVER THE POWER OF OUR SOFTWARE TODAY!
> In today's fast-paced digital world, robust tools play a pivotal role in boosting your team's productivity. It's not just software — it's a catalyst for transformation — a true game-changer that centralizes task tracking during your 14-day free trial. Our platform is scalable, robust, and secure, unifying scattered spreadsheets into one dashboard.
> [START YOUR FREE TRIAL NOW]

### After
> Centralize task tracking during your 14-day free trial.
> The platform unifies scattered spreadsheets into one dashboard.
> [Start free trial]

### What changed
- **No ALL CAPS for headings, warnings, or buttons**: replaced the shouted headline and button with sentence case.
- **Staged openers**: cut "In today's fast-paced digital world" and led with the product's actual capability.
- **Ban documented AI-vocabulary words**: removed "robust" and "pivotal."
- **Undocumented words**: removed "game-changer," a house-style word cut even though no official source attests it.
- **False contrasts**: dropped the chained "it's not just X, it's Y" construction.
- **Forced triads**: removed "scalable, robust, and secure," a three-part claim with no evidence behind it.
- **Cap em-dash use; do not ban it**: eliminated the em-dash chain used as a rhetorical connector.

## Example 2: Internal operations notice

### Before
> ATTENTION TEAM: PLEASE MAKE SURE YOU REVIEW THE CONFIGURATION BEFORE DEPLOYING.
> Hi everyone, hope this finds you well. Based on the latest security update, it is important to note that deployments to production require explicit approval from the on-call lead, no exceptions. At the end of the day, we are all responsible for keeping production stable. Please review the audit log and confirm that environment variables match the updated schema before submitting pull requests. In summary, let's keep production safe.

### After
> **Notice:** New requirements for production deployments
>
> Following the latest security update, deployments to production require explicit approval from the on-call lead, with no exceptions. Review the audit log and confirm that environment variables match the updated schema before submitting pull requests.

### What changed
- **No ALL CAPS for headings, warnings, or buttons**: replaced the shouted opening line with a sentence-case notice label.
- **No "please" by default**: dropped both instances of "please" from the instructions.
- **Staged openers**: cut "Hi everyone, hope this finds you well."
- **Cut dated disclaimer filler**: removed "it is important to note that."
- **Unverified filler phrases**: cut "At the end of the day," which added no information.
- **Cut stock conclusions**: removed "In summary" and ended on the concrete instruction instead.
